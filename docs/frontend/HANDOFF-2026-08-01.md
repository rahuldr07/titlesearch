## 6. The visual direction (the current reference)

Chosen hybrid, owner's words: **"A theme, B simplicity, C font, sidebar from A."**

- **Palette / depth / sidebar — A "Archival."** Warm stacked paper tones as the
  depth mechanism, low soft shadows instead of hairline borders, sealing-wax
  accent `#8A3222`.
- **Density and plainness — B "Precision Instrument."** Row padding 11×18
  (queue), 9×15 (decision), 8×16 (matrix). Where A is decorative and B is plain,
  **prefer B.**
- **Typography — C "Editorial."** Fraunces display (h1, order numbers, the
  italic decision question), Switzer body/UI, Geist Mono for every value and
  cite with `tnum`, Courier Prime inside the facsimile only.

Reference artefacts, **now in the repo** (2026-08-03 — they lived only in a
session scratchpad until then, which meant the app's actual visual reference was
one cleanup away from existing only as prose in this file):
`docs/frontend/directions/{a-archival,b-instrument,c-editorial,hybrid}.html` and
their `-full.png` renders. **The approved one is `hybrid.html`** — open that, not
the export, when checking whether something is right.

Fraunces deliberately stays OUT of dense tables — its figures are not tabular and
it is a display voice, not a text economy. The clients matrix proves the system.

## 9. 2026-08-03 — the rail rebuilt as A draws it

The reskin had landed A's palette on the navigator but not its anatomy. Against
the approved hybrid the rail was 232px not 264, rows 40px not 28, marks were
bordered letter squares rather than borderless pictographs, the active row was a
solid slab of the chip tint instead of a wash that fades, and the six-step spine
drew as 8px dashes floating between the discs rather than one line they hang on.

**Gate at the end:** 597 unit / 90 files · 104 e2e / 16 files · `check:rules`
clean over 376 files · typecheck clean · lint 0 errors. `git diff` on
`apps/web-v2/e2e` is **48 insertions, 0 deletions** — one test added, nothing
weakened.

**Two defects only a screenshot found, both green in 594 tests:**

- The spine painted **over** five of the six discs. Each segment spans from the
  previous disc's centre to its own, so its top half sits over the previous row —
  whose wrapper comes earlier in the DOM. At `z-index: auto` the line won. Only
  the active disc looked right, because nothing was drawn over it.
- Rows were 32px, not 28, and **no padding value was wrong.** `tokens.css`
  overrides Tailwind's `--text-*` SIZES without their paired line-heights, so
  `text-lg` at 13px inherited the stock 1.556 ratio meant for Tailwind's own `lg`
  step. **Any dense row in this app is exposed to this** — written up at the
  `--text-*` block in `tokens.css`.
