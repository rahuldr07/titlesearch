The direction · C — Editorial

The screen is set like a page: rules and a typographic grid do the structuring, and boxes are rationed to the two moments that deserve a frame — the next order and the open decision.

Typefaces

  • Fraunces — display: H1, order numbers, the question, notes in italic
  • Switzer — body, navigation, prose
  • Geist Mono — every value, cite, count, and label; tabular figures throughout
  • Courier Prime — the scanned page only

Palette & load-bearing decisions

  • #F7F3EA paper   #1C1915 ink   #1E3D5C navy   #A9391E stop   #8A6200 hold   #272119 surround
  • 1 · Hierarchy by rule weight: 2px ink opens a band, 1px hairline separates rows — no cards, no fills.
  • 2 · No-value states are carried by border grammar, colour-blind by design: dashed = expected absent, hatch = present-unreadable, dotted = document silent, flat grey = pending.
  • 3 · A value without a cite renders as an error (NO SOURCE), never as an ordinary value.
  • 4 · The scanned page keeps its own light paper on a dark surround — the photograph is never themed.

What we did not do

No gradients, no shadows on UI (only under the photographed page), no rates or timers anywhere, and no third accent — red and amber are spent only where the pipeline has actually stopped or is holding.
