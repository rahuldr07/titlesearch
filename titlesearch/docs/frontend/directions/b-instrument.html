The direction — B · Precision instrument

Type
  • Geist (300–800, variable) — every piece of UI. Hierarchy is weight × size × tracking, never a second face.
  • Geist Mono — order IDs, values, counts, times, citations. Tabular figures everywhere (tnum).
  • Courier inside the facsimile only — it is a photograph of paper, not UI.

Load-bearing decisions
  • The accent appears at full strength exactly once per screen — Take next order and Confirm as read. Everything else earns hierarchy from near-black, weight and tone.
  • No-value states are a border-and-pattern grammar, legible in greyscale: hatch = expected absent · dashed = searched-not-found · double+dots = document silent · smear = present-unreadable · dotted = pending.
  • A value without a citation renders as an error (red row, NO SOURCE chip) — provenance is structural, not decorative.
  • Layering by tone, not hairline soup: near-black rail → cool ground → raised panels, with a strict 2 / 4 / 6 px radius progression.
Deliberately not done
  • No serif, no gradients, no illustration, no rates or timers — and no colour anywhere it isn't a statement about the document or the one action that moves work forward.
