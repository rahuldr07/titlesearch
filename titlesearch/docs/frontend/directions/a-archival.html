The direction

A — Archival

Typefaces

  • Newsreader — human judgement: headings, questions, reasons, addresses
  • Switzer — UI furniture: labels, nav, buttons, chips
  • Geist Mono — machine output: values, order numbers, cites, counts
  • Courier Prime — the scanned page only

Palette

  • #E3DCCA ground · #F1ECDF sheet · #FFFDF6 card
  • #221C13 ink · #383129 surround
  • #8A3222 sealing-wax accent
  • #45714B confirmed · #96690F attention

Load-bearing decisions

  • Voice by typeface: serif = a person, mono = the machine, grotesque = the room. You always know who is talking.
  • Depth without borders: five paper tones stacked with low shadows; hairlines survive only inside components.
  • An absence grammar: dotted / solid-fill / dashed / dot-fill / hatch — five states legible with the colour removed.
  • One accent, spent deliberately: sealing-wax red marks the current thing, the stamp, and the error — nothing else.

Deliberately not done: no antique pastiche — no ornaments, ledger rules, or aged textures beyond the scan itself; the archive is the subject, not the costume.
